# Trademarks

The R4A source code is open under the [MIT License](LICENSE). You are free to use it, change it and share it.

The MIT License covers the code. It does not cover these:

- the name **"R4A"** (and "Rust for Android" used as the app's name),
- the **R4A logo and app icon**,
- **Ferrdroid**, the mascot (its name, look and animations).

## What this means for you

- **Forks and modified builds** are welcome. Please give them **a different name and a different icon**, so people can tell them apart from the official R4A.
- You can still say what your project is based on, for example *"based on R4A"* or *"a fork of R4A"*. That's fine and appreciated.
- Writing about R4A, reviewing it, or linking to it is always fine, with screenshots and the logo included.
- Please don't publish builds that look like the official app, or suggest that the R4A project made or endorses them.

The official downloads are listed in the [README](README.md#where-to-get-the-official-version).
